Free STEM Tutoring for North Carolina Students

CarolinaSTEM is a student-led nonprofit dedicated to making quality STEM education accessible to all North Carolina middle and high school students through free tutoring and engaging outreach that inspires the next generation of scientists, engineers, and innovators.

Request Tutoring Volunteer With Us

Why It Matters

Many students in North Carolina face challenges accessing high-quality STEM support outside the classroom. CarolinaSTEM bridges that gap by connecting students with peer tutors who provide personalized guidance, help build confidence, and spark curiosity in science and math. Our programs give every student the tools to succeed and explore their STEM potential.

More About Us

Intro to Tutoring

Our flagship tutoring program connects middle and high school students across North Carolina with volunteer tutors who provide personalized, one-on-one support in math and science. Each session is tailored to the student's learning style and pace, helping them build confidence and develop a genuine interest in STEM.
