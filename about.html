About CarolinaSTEM

Learn more about our mission, vision, and the passionate team behind CarolinaSTEM

Our Mission

CarolinaSTEM is a student-led nonprofit organization founded with a simple yet powerful mission: to make quality STEM education accessible to all middle and high school students across North Carolina, regardless of their background or geographic location.

We believe every student has the potential to excel in STEM when given the right support and resources. Through our free tutoring and outreach programs, we bridge educational gaps and inspire the next generation of innovators, problem-solvers, and leaders.

Our Vision

We envision a North Carolina where every student, regardless of their location or income, has access to high-quality STEM education and mentorship. We see a future where barriers that prevent students from pursuing STEM careers are removed through community support and collaborative learning.

Our long-term vision includes expanding our reach statewide, developing innovative educational resources, and creating pathways for students to transition from tutees to tutors fostering a sustainable cycle of peer support and learning.

Our Story

CarolinaSTEM was founded in 2025 by a group of North Carolina students passionate about making STEM education more accessible. After seeing how many middle and high schoolers in North Carolina struggle to find free, personalized help in math and science, we decided to create a program that connects students with volunteer tutors who care about their success. Right now, we're just getting started, but our goal is simple: to make quality STEM support available to every student in NC who needs it.
