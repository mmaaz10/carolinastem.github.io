Free STEM Tutoring

Our flagship tutoring program connects middle and high school students across North Carolina with qualified volunteer tutors who provide personalized, one-on-one academic support in mathematics and science subjects.

✓

Personalized Learning

Each tutoring session is tailored to the individual student's learning style, pace, and specific needs.

✓

Consistent, Structured Sessions

Our tutoring sessions are held on set days each week to ensure consistency and progress. Families can choose from our available time slots that best fit their schedules.
